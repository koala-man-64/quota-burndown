How to read the ordering

  1. P0 exists for live fires, and its contents rotate. A single recorded predecessor link from a bug can park an entire program — as 2557 and 2699 did to Regime Forecasting until they closed — and data-starvation bugs act as de facto gates even with no link recorded. This list describes the ordering logic only; the current live status (what's open, resolved, or newly on fire) lives in the tiles at the top, which are refreshed with every pull.
  2. P1 is three independent tracks — runtime resilience (F2598), migration completion (F2599), and delivery-truth hygiene (2612/2613, mesh policy) — they share no dependencies and can be worked by three agents/streams concurrently.
  3. P2 is the highest-leverage column on the board. The July 28 grading found the governed approval pipeline unreachable by construction: five canonical gates have zero producers. 2609, 2610, 2611 and 2614 are deliberately small stories that give each gate its first producer — and they are recorded predecessors of the two hardest AA 2.0 features. Do these before any AA 2.0 feature work.
  4. P3 items are the entry tickets to AA 2.0. Each maps to exactly one AA 2.0 feature (1232→2095, 291→2096, 302→2099, 257+2022→2097, 265+1180→2098). They are mutually independent — parallelize freely.
  5. AA 2.0 features unlock in three waves, now shown as separate columns: P4 foundations (2092, 2093, 2095, 2096, 2100 — all gated only on P0–P3 work, all parallel) → P5 evidence & workstation (2094, 2099 — need the P2 evidence stories) → P6 engine then certification (2097, then 2098, which needs the paper book's live-forward clock to have been running). 2098 is the end of the critical path.
  6. Regime Forecasting is your parallel lane — a different team, sequential phases 0→1→2→3, only touching the main path at 2557 (start) and 2375/2390 (which later unlock Clustering).
  7. Clustering and Commodity stay parked. Clustering's first features have recorded predecessors in Regime Phase 2; Commodity is tagged queued-behind-aa-2.0. Starting either early spends capacity ahead of their gates.
